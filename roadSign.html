Road Signs Tutorial

Master traffic signs to become a safer and more confident driver

⚠️ Warning Signs

These signs alert drivers to potential hazards or changing road conditions ahead. They are usually diamond-shaped with a yellow background and black symbols. → Explore Warning Signs

🚫 Prohibitory Signs

These signs indicate actions that drivers are not allowed to do, such as no entry, no overtaking, no parking, or no U-turns. They are typically circular with a red border. → Explore Prohibitory Signs

⭕Restrictive Signs

These signs place limits or restrictions on drivers, such as speed limits, no parking zones, or weight restrictions. They are often circular with red borders. → Explore Restrictive Signs

➡️ Mandatory Signs

These signs indicate actions that must be done, such as direction to follow or requirements like wearing seat belts. Mostly blue circles with white symbols. → Explore Mandatory Signs

🛑 Priority Signs

These signs indicate who has the right of way in specific traffic situations. Drivers must obey them to prevent accidents and ensure smooth traffic flow. → Explore Guide Signs
